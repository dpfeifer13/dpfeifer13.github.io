About Design Forge | Elon University Center for Design Thinking

In my role as a Video Production Assistant at Elon University, I helped produce a video for Design Forge, an annual conference organized by the Center for Design Thinking. My duties involved capturing extensive footage of the event, assisting with interviews, and collaborating closely with the event organizers to ensure the final product aligned with their vision.

The goal of this project was to create a short piece that showcased the lively atmosphere of Design Forge and featured enthusiastic testimonials from attendees. By documenting the event and interviewing speakers and organizers, we aimed to provide an engaging preview of the conference, with the goal of encouraging even greater participation from the academic community in the future.

Project Scope

  • Video capture
  • Lighting assistance
  • Audio capture
  • Client coordination
