Can a Middle Power Run Its Own Grand Strategy? | Eye on Korea Ep. 40

Eye on Korea is the Korea Economic Institute of America’s interview series on the politics and economics shaping the Korean peninsula.

In this episode, Dr. Andrew Yeo argues that South Korea is running a deliberate grand strategy — pursuing autonomy and freedom of action while keeping the U.S.–South Korea alliance intact, and turning the trade power and influence it has built into real leverage. He lays out what that strategy looks like in practice, and whether a country constrained by larger powers can actually pull it off.

I handle production and post for the series, cutting each long-form conversation into the published episode and maintaining its on-screen graphics package.
