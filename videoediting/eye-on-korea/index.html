Eye on Korea

Eye on Korea is the Korea Economic Institute of America’s flagship video podcast — a bi-weekly interview series on the politics, economics and security questions shaping the Korean peninsula. Guests range from former ambassadors and senior officials to leading academics and policy analysts.

I produce the series end to end: recording logistics and guest coordination ahead of each taping, multi-camera capture in studio, and the edit that turns a long, dense conversation into a published episode — on a standing two-week cycle, every cycle.
