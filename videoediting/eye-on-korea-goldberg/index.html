South Korea Has a New President — What Comes Next? | Eye on Korea Ep. 12

KEI President Scott Snyder sits down with Ambassador Philip Goldberg, former U.S. Ambassador to South Korea, for a wide-ranging conversation on South Korea’s political transformation following the election of president Lee Jae-myung, and what it means for foreign policy and U.S.–ROK relations.

Ambassador Goldberg reflects candidly on the final months of his tenure in Seoul — including the declaration of martial law and the impeachment proceedings against former president Yoon Suk Yeol.

I produce Eye on Korea for KEI, handling recording logistics and guest coordination alongside the edit. A sit-down with a former ambassador leaves no room for a second take, so the priority was a clean multi-camera capture and a cut that keeps a long, dense conversation watchable without flattening the nuance.
