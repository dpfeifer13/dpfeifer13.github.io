Utility Cart Training Course — Operating the Cart

To address the need for an updated training resource, I was tasked with scripting and helping to plan a comprehensive video series designed to train faculty and staff at Elon University on the proper use of utility carts. This series covers essential topics such as daily safety inspections, safe operation principles, driving guidelines, personal safety measures, and parking protocols.

My contributions included the scripting and copywriting, pre-production planning, and incorporating the Phoenix sports mascot to enhance engagement. During production, I also assisted by coordinating logistics and ensuring the team captured the necessary shots. This project showcases my ability to write and produce informational content and collaborate effectively in a production environment.

Project Scope

  • Scriptwriting
  • Video assistance
  • Production management
