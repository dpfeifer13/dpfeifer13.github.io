United Methodist Church in Madison — “Welcome Video”

As a freelance video producer this past summer, I was hired to create this video for the United Methodist Church in Madison, NJ. For this project, I was tasked with capturing and conveying the spirit of inclusivity that the church embodies. I chose to highlight key moments during services that showcased genuine moments of connection and interaction among church members, emphasizing the church’s welcoming message and its diverse congregation. Additionally, by capturing footage of both the church and its surrounding town, I aimed to underscore the church’s connection with the community.

The final video, now a prominent feature in the church’s online services and on its website, not only portrays the physical beauty of the church but also effectively communicates its atmosphere of openness and inclusivity.

Project Scope

  • Video capture
  • Video editing
  • Script assistance
  • Audio capture
