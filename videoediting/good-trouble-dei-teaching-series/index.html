Good Trouble: DEI Focused Teaching Series

I shot and edited this video for Elon University’s “Good Trouble: DEI Focused Teaching Series” and wrote the copy for the introduction and closing segments.

This episode, which focuses on neurodiversity, is part of a series that explores strategies for incorporating Justice, Equity, Diversity, Inclusion, and Belongingness into the classroom. The series features a sci-fi theme to offer educators practical tips for fostering more inclusive learning environments.
