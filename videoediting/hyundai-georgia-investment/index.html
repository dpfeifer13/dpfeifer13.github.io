Georgia’s Largest Investment Ever: Inside Hyundai’s $7.6B Megaproject

Part of Inside the Investment, a documentary series for the Korea Economic Institute of America examining how Korean capital is reshaping American communities.

This episode goes inside Hyundai Motor Group’s $7.6 billion Metaplant — the largest economic development project in Georgia’s history. Anchored by the plant, the state has become a hub for advanced production, high-skilled employment and supply-chain resilience, and the film traces what that shift looks like on the ground.

I create the Inside the Investment mini-documentaries for KEI — filming South Korean investment sites across the U.S., shooting the interviews and on-site footage, and shaping the story in post-production.

Project Scope

  • Field production
  • Interview filming
  • Video editing
  • Post-production
