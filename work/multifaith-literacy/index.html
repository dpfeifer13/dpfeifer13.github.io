Multifaith Literacy in the Workplace

As a Video Production Assistant for Teaching and Learning Technologies at Elon University, I worked on this promotional video for a new course series on Multifaith Literacy in the Workplace, offered through Elon Next. My responsibilities on this project encompassed assisting with filming, managing equipment setup and operation, and editing the final product. The final video is now being used by the university to advertise the course across social media platforms.
