Live Events

The Korea Economic Institute of America runs public programming out of its Washington office: panels, policy discussions and commemorations, streamed live to an audience that is often larger online than in the room.

I direct the live video coverage as part of a two-person team, managing the livestream and running multi-camera production. There is no second take on a live show, so the work is in the preparation: camera positions, audio, and calling the cut while a conversation is happening in front of you.

The stream above is a panel of former U.S. ambassadors on the election of president Lee Jae-myung and what it means for U.S.–Korea relations.

Project Scope

  • Live direction
  • Multi-camera production
  • Livestream management
  • Audio and camera setup
